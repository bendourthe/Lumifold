# Known Gaps -- v0.5.0

Open items and platform limits for the Duo effect overhaul. Counts: 8 open, 0 closed.

## Open

1. **Cover keep-awake not yet verified on hardware.** The mechanism follows AOSP `BookStyleClosedStatePredicate` (screen timeout policy `KEEP_DISPLAY_ON` keeps CLOSED up to about 90 degrees), but it has only been exercised on an emulator, whose foldable profile does not swap displays. Check on a Pixel foldable running Android 16: open from the cover home screen and read "Screen switch when opening" in the app's diagnostics. A value near 90 confirms it; a value near 0 means the device does not apply the policy to wallpaper-held wake locks.
2. **Android 15 and earlier switch displays immediately.** No public API lets an app delay the CLOSED to HALF_OPENED transition, so the cover phase is only visible for the first few degrees. The inner display still runs its half of the effect.
3. **Wake lock re-arm only sees wallpaper interaction.** Taps on icons or widgets reset the system's screen timeout but not the wallpaper's lock, so after a long session of launcher use the lock may already have expired when the hinge opens.
4. **Viewer position is assumed.** The shared-image-plane projection assumes an eye 30 cm in front of the stationary half. Off-axis viewing shows a slight mismatch at the crease during the transition.
5. **Photo resolution.** The 12 bundled photos have a short side of 1333 to 2000 px, so a near-square inner display about 2150 px tall crops and upscales them by up to 1.6x. Generative designs render at native resolution. Sourcing 4K originals would close this gap.
6. **Inner display rotation.** Landscape inner layouts map the moving half through the display rotation, which is unit-tested but not yet checked on hardware.
7. **Motion maps are rule-based.** Landscape motion maps come from color, brightness, smoothness and position rules, not segmentation. Small misclassified areas (for example bright cliff faces near the sky, rocks in a river) can drift or ripple slightly. Hand-painting the maps or adding photos with cleaner regions would remove this.
8. **Emulator performance.** On the software-GPU emulator the animated 3D preview and live wallpaper run below 60 fps. Thumbnails are cached so the app stays responsive; frame rate on hardware has not been measured.
