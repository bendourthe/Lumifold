# Known Gaps -- v0.5.0

Open items and platform limits for the Duo effect overhaul, reconciled at the v0.5.0 release on October 28, 2026. Status: finalized. Counts: 3 resolved, 1 resolved in part, 2 accepted limits, 3 deferred to v0.6.

## Resolved

1. **Cover keep-awake verified on hardware.** On a Pixel 11 Pro Fold running Android 17 the cover stays on until about 90 degrees while opening, on Home and over apps (T107), and closing hands over to the cover below about 45 degrees.
7. **Landscape content shipped.** Landscapes, cities and scenes play real video loops: 63 clips are hosted on the `media-v1` release and downloaded on demand (T079).
9. **Photo licenses recorded.** Every shipped photograph has its source, author and license in [docs/reference/photo-credits.md](../../../../reference/photo-credits.md); rows for removed wallpapers were dropped at this release.

## Resolved in Part

3. **Wake lock re-arm.** With the Lumifold accessibility service enabled, a 1x1 touch watch re-arms the cover hold on any touch, over apps and on Home. Without it, only touches on the wallpaper re-arm the lock, so after a long session of launcher use the lock may already have expired when the hinge opens. Suggested next step: none; enabling the service is the supported path.

## Accepted Limits

2. **Android 15 and earlier switch displays immediately.** No public API lets an app delay the CLOSED to HALF_OPENED transition, so the cover phase is only visible for the first few degrees. The inner display still runs its half of the effect.
4. **Viewer position is assumed.** The shared-image-plane projection assumes an eye in front of the stationary half. Off-axis viewing shows a slight mismatch at the crease during the transition. Full swipe, the default, keeps the picture in place and is unaffected.

## Deferred to v0.6

5. **Photo resolution.** Not re-measured for this release. Some bundled photos may still be upscaled on the near-square inner display. Suggested next step: list photos whose short side is below the inner display's height and source larger originals.
6. **Inner display rotation.** Landscape inner layouts map the moving half through the display rotation, which is unit-tested but not yet checked on hardware. Suggested next step: rotate the opened phone to landscape on Home and over an app and confirm the effect direction.
8. **Frame rate on hardware.** Not measured. Suggested next step: record frame timing with `dumpsys gfxinfo` during an opening on Home and over an app.
