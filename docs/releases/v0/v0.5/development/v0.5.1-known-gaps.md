# Known Gaps -- v0.5.1

Open items and platform limits for the setup, strength and cover-reveal release, reconciled on October 29, 2026. Status: finalized. Counts: 1 open, 2 accepted limits, 3 still deferred to v0.6 from [v0.5.0](v0.5.0-known-gaps.md).

## Open

1. **Setup prompt not yet seen on hardware.** The prompt appears only while "display over other apps" or the Lumifold accessibility service is off, and both were on during device testing. Its logic is shared with Advanced settings, which were verified. Suggested next step: turn off "display over other apps" for Lumifold, reopen the app, and confirm the prompt and both buttons.

## Accepted Limits

2. **Android's panel switch dims the cover briefly.** While closing, Android brings the cover up in a dimmed or frozen state during its screen switch, and an app cannot change that transition. Lumifold starts the cover from a matching covered pose and reveals it from there, so the switch reads as part of the effect rather than being removed.
3. **No blur over apps.** Android blurs another app's window only in uniform rectangles, whose hard edges would cut across the sweep, so the effect over apps dims and does not blur.

## Deferred to v0.6

Items 5 (photo resolution), 6 (inner display rotation) and 8 (frame rate on hardware) from the [v0.5.0 known gaps](v0.5.0-known-gaps.md) are unchanged by this release and stay deferred with the same suggested next steps.
